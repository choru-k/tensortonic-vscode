# 0.12.6 cloud-validated candidate

[Download the 0.12.6 VSIX](tensortonic-vscode-0.12.6.vsix).

This candidate adds the entire closed signed-in top navigation capsule to Focus mode, while preserving separate Problem/Theory/Solution/Submissions/Notes/Code tabs. A conservative observer reopens the capsule on any relevant DOM mutation, including unlabelled notices or replacement navigation. Benign UI updates may reopen it too; changed content is never automatically hidden again.

The final source passed 800/800 serial aggregate tests, 102/102 session checks and 76/76 transport checks. Independent source and packaging review passed. ZIP integrity and all 61 packaged source/document/asset files were verified against the reviewed source.

Authenticated native rendering and actual Focus toggle/refresh behavior remain unverified for 0.12.6. A cloud Chromium attempt was blocked before launch by environment socket restrictions. Publication does not mean installation or native acceptance. See [release validation](../RELEASE-VALIDATION.md) and [capsule evidence and limits](../docs/focus-capsule-validation.md).
